## FAQ

**Where do listings come from?**  
Direct company ATS boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Oracle, iCIMS, and others), USAJOBS, hardware feeds, and community submissions — refreshed hourly.

**How do I know a role is still open?**  
Open roles have an Apply link. Closed roles show 🔒. Links are checked regularly.

**Visa / citizenship?**  
🛂 = no sponsorship · 🇺🇸 = U.S. citizenship required. If neither flag appears, check the posting — status may be unknown.

**Found a non-EE role or a mistake?**  
Open an issue or PR. This list stays EE-only on purpose.
